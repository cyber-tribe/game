Character design sheet (settei-gasho) for a Japanese RPG video game art book, in the style of 1990s Japanese fantasy manga/anime illustration: confident clean ink linework, cel-shaded painted colors with soft shadows, timid but charming mood.

Single page layout on parchment-white background containing:

CREATURE: A small four-legged rodent creature, born from a fleeting flicker of anxiety in a sleeping giant's dream. Cowardly — it gnaws on things to calm itself, and flees the moment it is hurt. Small, low to the ground, a bit less than one head tall.
