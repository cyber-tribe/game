Character design sheet (settei-gasho) for a Japanese RPG video game art book, in the style of 1990s Japanese fantasy manga/anime illustration: confident clean ink linework, cel-shaded painted colors with soft shadows, warm and adventurous mood.

Single page layout on parchment-white background containing:
- LEFT SIDE, dominant: one full-body standing pose of the character, realistic human proportions (about 6.5-7 heads tall boy), relaxed confident stance, front three-quarter view.
- RIGHT SIDE: a column of smaller studies — 3 head/face studies from different angles (front, three-quarter, profile) showing his hairstyle clearly, and one small study of his hands and one of his back showing the small wooden barrel he carries on his back with two shoulder straps.

CHARACTER: A village boy around 12-13 years old, an apprentice barrel-keeper. Honest, stubborn, straightforward personality; slightly reckless but kind eyes. NOT chibi, NOT super-deformed — realistic human anatomy and proportions for his age.

APPEARANCE:
- Short tousled brown hair, sun-tanned skin, thick determined eyebrows, bright alert eyes.
- Amber/caramel colored sturdy work jacket (hand-woven rustic fabric), collar folded, sleeves rolled to the elbows.
- Grey-blue rough work trousers, tucked into short leather boots.
- A red braided cord tied somewhere visible (wrist or waist) as an accent color.
- On his back: a small wooden barrel (like a keg) held by two leather shoulder straps; iron hoop bands on the barrel. The barrel is his tool of trade — he catches dream-creatures in it.
- A wide work belt with small pouches.

The overall look: a working village kid from a warm mountain village, practical worn clothing, nothing royal or knightly. No weapons except maybe a small wooden mallet at the belt.

Clean game-artbook presentation, no text, no logos, no watermark, white/parchment background, all studies drawn in the same consistent style and palette.
